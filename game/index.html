Wildfire Air Tanker

Three fires are burning in the valley. Take off from the strip, skim the lake low and slow to fill the belly tank, drop the water on the flames, then bring her home and land. Watch your fuel — the valley is wide and the fires do not wait.

The sortie

  1. Take off: full throttle on the runway, pull up past 30 m/s.
  2. Scoop: fly 2–9 m over the lake at 25–50 m/s — the tank fills, spray flies. Touch the water and you ditch.
  3. Drop: the amber reticle ahead marks where the water will land. Open the doors over a fire and watch it shrink.
  4. Land: all fires out, line up on the runway, gentle descent, roll to a stop. Stopped on the strip, fuel refills.

Below 24 m/s the wing stalls and she sinks. Fuel empty means a glide — pick your field, or better, your runway.
